BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

What should I include?

If you're reporting a problem, providing a little extra information can make it much easier to figure out what's going on.

  • Which ALCB Games project you're having trouble with
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Any error messages you received
