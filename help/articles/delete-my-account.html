How to delete my account and information from ALCB Servers.

We make it very easy to delete all of your info, so you can have full control over your privacy. This helps build a safe enviornment for everybody, including yourself. Want to come back later? Simply sign up again!

⚠️ WARNING

Once you delete your account all of your information are deleted PERMANENTLY, all cloud-synced data cannot be recovered.

How to delete your account/info.

Send us an email at alcbgames@outlook.com and we will help you follow the simple verification steps. By contacting us, you agree that you have full access to the account, to make verification simplier, please email us off the email used on your account.
