Welcome!

Welcome to Astronomically Large Cheeseburger Games. This support center is here to help you find information about our games, troubleshoot problems, and figure out where to go when you need additional help.

Having a problem?

If something isn't working correctly, start by checking the support articles for information about the issue. We may already have a solution available.

💡 Tip

When reporting an issue, include useful information such as what you were doing when the problem occurred and what happened afterward.

Still need help?

If you can't find an answer here, head over to the Support page.
