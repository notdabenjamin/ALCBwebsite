How to avoid phishing & spoofing

How to protect your ALCB account from phishing & spoofing, and what you can do to help others.

How to avoid phishing/spoofing.

1. Always check the url before logging into a website. If you notice the website url is even a little off from the one you 100% know is offical, RUN AWAY. Scammers will attempt to make the website look as offical as possible. 2. No matter how clean, how good, how well made the website looks it could still very much be a trap, scammers know the more styled the website looks the more people will think it's real. 3. Don't click on links from people you don't trust. 4. If your friend sends you a website link, still check it, they could be hacked or just evil. 5. Not all spam goes in the spam folder, scammers can easily trick you and your inbox.

💡Tip: Reporting malicious links

If you've found a suspicious looking website and need to report it, go to https://phish.report/ and follow the instructions. If it is related to ALCB and attemping to steal your ALCB account, please send us an email!
